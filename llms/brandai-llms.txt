# Brand.ai

> Brand.ai (Access to Tools Inc.) is a San Francisco AI platform for brand management that turns brand assets into machine-readable intelligence, so people and AI agents can keep brand consistency across tools and workflows. Founded 2024, backed by Uncork Capital. It is a demo-gated SaaS product — no public API, SDK, or developer portal at this time.

## Products
- [Brand OS](https://brand.ai/brand-os/): Structures brand data (Brand Foundation, Brand Ontology, Daily Signals, connected apps) into machine-readable format.
- [Brand Studio](https://brand.ai/brand-studio/): Asset creation, visual strategy Canvas, project management, and Brand Check validation.
